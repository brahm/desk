# Phase W4: Writer, objects and charts of 15

The plan of phase W4 of the [roadmap](../roadmap.md), written when it
started on 2026-11-27. It follows "How a phase runs": the rows of the
phase, the parity rows that test each one, what is missing, how each is
checked, and the order of the pull requests. It is corrected as the phase
goes and closed with it.

The phase owns 20 rows of [`corpus/inventory/writer.tsv`](../../corpus/inventory/writer.tsv)
(W106 to W124 and W141 to W146) and 1 item of the
[backlog](../backlog/deferred.tsv) (B-013, from B1). The roadmap
counted 21 rows, one more than the inventory gives the phase, and is
corrected to 20 in this pull request. Desk follows GNOME where it and iWork differ (D-022), and keeps
what Pages '09 did inside a shared feature (D-003).

## What reading the rows showed

- Four rows are done and only need the check of the new target, since
  Pages 15.4 changed them: text wrap (W107), the Instant Alpha of the '09
  in place of the Remove Background of the 15, which uses AI (W109, D-003,
  D-004), axes, labels and legends (W143) and the 3D scene (W146). The
  last two also need the check of the new window (D-008).
- Eleven rows exist as Pages '09 had them and need what Pages 15.4 adds:
  objects inside boxes and shapes (W106), SVG and HEIC images (W108),
  Enhance and the lean panel of image adjustments (W110), the searchable
  shape library and combining shapes (W113), recording, trimming, the
  poster frame and the description of sound and video (W118), optimizing
  media on insert (W119), the advanced gradient and saved fills (W121),
  the colour of the guides (W124), the donut, radar, bubble and
  interactive charts (W141), sharing the X values of a chart (W142) and
  reference lines (W144). Their parity rows, `OBJ-01` to `OBJ-42` and
  `GRF-01` to `GRF-20`, were written for Pages '09 and carry the old
  phases 6 and 7.
- Five rows are missing: the image gallery (W111), 3D objects (W116),
  drawings (W117, the Apple Pencil of iPad, which on GNOME is the pointer
  or a stylus, D-005), captions and titles of objects (W122) and the
  rounded corners, bevel and donut hole of charts (W145). None has a
  parity row.
- The colour of the guides of W124 is already a preference of Desk
  (`guide-color`, `desk-ui/src/preferences.rs`), made in B1; the row may
  only need its check and its status.
- Generating a shape with AI (W113), the Content Hub and the photos of an
  iPhone (W108) and video from the web (W118) stay out (D-004, D-005).
- B-013, the text of one series, axis or legend formatted apart from the
  rest of the chart (the rest of `GRF-08`), came to W4 with the chart
  styles of the 15, which W1 made (W147).
- Three rows need a library Desk does not have yet, each from the GNOME
  runtime and chosen in its pull request: the drawing of SVG (librsvg)
  and the reading of HEIC (glycin, which Loupe uses, or libheif), and
  recording sound (GStreamer, which plays media already through GTK).
- 3D objects (W116) are the least known: no Rust crate reads USDZ, and
  GNOME has no viewer of 3D models. The owner decided what Desk opens
  (D-033): USDZ, as Pages 15.4 does, and glTF and GLB, U3D and PRC (the
  3D of PDF documents), FBX and OBJ (the 3D of Microsoft Office), each
  with a reader of its own. The session of step 2 sees what Pages 15.4
  does with them.

## What step 3 changed

Step 3 goes in three pull requests: objects in text boxes and shapes,
then in table cells, then titles and captions (W122).

- Pictures, shapes and equations pasted while the text of a text box or
  a shape is being edited go in its lines at the cursor, as the Pages
  User Guide says of 15.4 ("Place objects inside a text box or shape").
  Other objects paste on the page, as before. A picture pasted from
  another app goes in the line too, no wider than the text of the box.
- The objects in the text of a box stand on the baseline of their line,
  which grows to hold them, and turn and move with the box. Copying the
  box copies them; deleting it deletes them. A mark in the text of its
  own object is not copied into itself again.
- In ODF they are `draw:frame` and shapes anchored `as-char` in the
  `text:p` of the `draw:text-box` or of the shape, as LibreOffice writes
  them; no extension is needed.
- Seen again at the end on the Mac: whether Insert > Choose and Insert >
  Equation put the object in the line when the cursor is in a text box,
  and whether dragging an object into a box nests it. Desk puts them on
  the page for now.
- Titles and captions (W122, PXV-24, #262): the Style tab turns on the
  Title, at the Top or the Bottom, and the Caption of the selected
  objects. Turned on, each holds its name in the paragraph style
  Caption, centered, which Desk makes when the document has none; a
  click on it edits it in place, and Undo takes the visit back as one
  action. Turned off, it keeps its text. They stand above and below the
  object at its width, upright when it turns, move with it, and the text
  around goes around them too; an object in the line grows its line by
  them, its caption on the baseline. They hold text only, no objects or
  notes. In ODF the object goes in a frame of text with its title and
  caption, as LibreOffice writes a caption, which `desk:captions` marks
  (docs/odf-extensions.md). Moved to W5 (B-081): those of the objects of
  master pages and page templates, and the text of one turned off, in
  ODF; and showing those of objects inside text boxes, shapes and
  groups, and of a text box that hangs in the line.
- Objects in table cells, the rest of W106, are their own step at the
  end of W4, as the owner chose on 27 November.

## What step 4 changed

- SVG and HEIC images (W108, OBJ-02): Insert > Choose File, dragging a
  file onto the page and opening an ODT or a Pages document take them
  as they are. An SVG is drawn by librsvg as lines and text, sharp at
  every size and in a PDF; it loads nothing from outside its own file.
  A HEIC photograph is decoded by libheif, turned as the camera saved
  it, and kept as HEIC, so that it stays small. A HEIC or AVIF file is
  no longer taken for a movie.
- In ODF each goes in its `draw:image` with its media type, followed by
  a second `draw:image` holding a PNG of it, at most 2048 pixels on its
  longer side, as LibreOffice writes an SVG for the programs that do not
  read it. On reading, the second picture stands for the first when Desk
  cannot read that one. ODF names both; no extension is needed.
- The Flatpak builds libheif and libde265, which the GNOME runtime does
  not have; HEIC files in AV1 (AVIF) are not decoded.
- Image adjustments (W110, OBJ-07): the Image tab shows Exposure and
  Saturation, each with a field and a slider, Enhance and Reset Image,
  as Pages 15.4 does. Enhance reads the histogram of the picture and
  sets the exposure halfway to a middle lightness, and more saturation
  for a dull picture; a gray one stays gray. Adjust Image… opens the
  full window of Pages '09 (D-003): the levels over the histogram, with
  Auto Levels, which takes the darkest and lightest values leaving out
  the outermost half percent, then brightness, contrast, saturation,
  exposure, temperature, tint and sharpness, each with a slider. All of
  it stays undoable and leaves the picture as it is. In ODF the
  sharpness and the levels are `desk:image-sharpness` and
  `desk:image-levels`, beside `desk:image-adjust`, which older versions
  of Desk keep reading. Enhance looks at the picture as the levels,
  brightness and contrast already set leave it.
- Image gallery (W111, PXV-23): Insert > Image Gallery puts an empty
  gallery, 320 by 240 points, in the middle of the page, drawn light
  gray until it has pictures. Pictures dropped on it, or added with the
  + of the Gallery tab, go after the one shown. While it is selected, two
  round arrows over it step back and forward, going around at the ends.
  The Gallery tab lists the pictures with a thumbnail, each shown, moved
  up or down, or taken out; turns on a caption for each picture instead
  of one for all; and sets the Image Description of the one shown. The
  title and the caption are turned on in the Style tab, as for any
  object (W122). The picture shown is drawn whole, centered in the
  frame. In ODF the frame holds a `draw:image` of the picture shown, a
  PNG for an SVG or a HEIC one, which other programs show, and a
  `desk:gallery` with the pictures, their descriptions and their
  captions. Pages reorders the list by dragging; Desk moves a picture
  with its up and down buttons, which a keyboard reaches too.
- Combining shapes (W113, PXV-27): Arrange > Shape has Unite, Intersect,
  Subtract and Exclude Shapes, as Format > Shapes and Lines of Pages
  15.4, and Break Apart. The shapes are combined as they are placed,
  rotated and flipped, curves made into short straight lines; the result
  takes the place and the look of the shape at the back. Subtract takes
  those in front out of the one at the back; Exclude leaves holes where
  an even number overlap. A result of one part is a shape drawn with the
  pen, whose points edit; one of several parts, or with a hole, is a
  shape of several parts, which Break Apart makes into one shape for
  each part, holes too, as Pages does. In ODF it is a custom shape whose
  enhanced path has a subpath for each part, so other programs draw it,
  with `desk:shape` `path compound`.
- Fills (W121): the advanced gradient was already in the Format pane
  (OBJ-41) with its colours, blend points, angle and radial kind;
  Reverse Colors now turns a gradient around, for two colours or more,
  each blend point kept where it was between its two colours. Saved
  Fills, at the end of the fill rows, keeps a colour, a gradient or an
  advanced gradient in `saved-fills.txt` of the data folder of Desk, to
  use again in any document, as the colour picker of Pages 15.4 keeps
  fills; a right click deletes one. A picture is not kept: it belongs to
  its document. Lines a later version wrote are kept when the file
  changes.
- Drawings (W117): Insert > Drawing, also at the bottom of the shape
  library, turns drawing on, and the pointer or a stylus draws, as the
  Apple Pencil draws in Pages on an iPad (D-005). The bar of drawing,
  under the header bar, holds the pen, the pencil, the crayon and the
  fill of Pages, the eraser, the colour, the width and Done. The lines
  go into one drawing, which grows to hold them and is drawn on again
  with a double click or Edit Drawing; each line is one step of Undo.
  The fill fills the area around the click that one line or several
  close, behind the lines around it, as the fill of Pages does. The Drawing
  tab animates the drawing, its lines drawn again in their order over a
  time, once or in a loop, and plays it on the page. ODT keeps a group
  of paths, which other programs show, with the tool of each path and
  the animation in `desk:drawing`.
- Sound and video (W118, W119): Insert > Record Audio records from the
  microphone through GStreamer, which plays media through GTK already,
  with the time, the loudness and Preview, and Insert puts the sound on
  the page as a speaker, as a WAVE file that Reduce Media cuts. The
  Movie and Sound group of the Format pane has a bar of the length,
  with the part that plays between two handles and the poster frame
  above it, all three dragged, over the Start, Stop and Poster Frame
  rows, which show the frame taken. The Description says what the movie
  or sound shows, kept as the `svg:desc` of its frame in ODT. Optimize
  for Phones and Tablets, and the preference Optimize Movies and Sounds
  on insert, off as it was, code a movie again into MPEG-4 at most 1280
  × 720, with the best encoder the computer has (H.264, MPEG-4 Part 2
  or VP9, with AAC or Opus), and a sound of samples into MP3; the file
  is kept only when it comes out smaller, and how it plays stays.
- The shape library (W113): the Shape button opens a library as that of
  Pages 15.4, with the categories at the side (Basic, Geometry, Arrows,
  Symbols, Nature, Objects and My Shapes), a grid of the shapes, and a
  search field that finds them in all the categories by their names and
  other words, whatever their case and accents. The shapes are drawn
  for Desk, 57 of them, none taken from Pages (D-011); Basic holds the
  shapes of Insert > Shape. Arrange > Shape > Save to My Shapes… keeps
  the selected shape, by a name, in `my-shapes.txt` of the data folder
  of Desk, as large as it was; a right click renames or deletes one.
  Pages keeps My Shapes in iCloud, which Desk has no part of. The pen
  and the connection line are at the bottom of the library.
- Still in W4, for the session on the Mac of step 13: what Auto Crop,
  seen beside Edit Mask in 15.4, takes off a picture, before Desk does
  it; the names under which a Pages '09 file keeps the sharpness and the
  levels, which Desk reads once a file shows them (it reads the six
  others today); and the checks of W107 (text wrap) and W109 (Instant
  Alpha).

## ODF

What ODF 1.3 names is kept in it: objects inline in boxes and shapes as
`draw:frame` anchored `as-char` in their text; SVG images as
`draw:image` with the type `image/svg+xml`, with a PNG beside it as
LibreOffice writes for other programs; the title and description of an
object as `svg:title` and `svg:desc`; a caption as the text of a frame
around the object, as LibreOffice does; combined shapes and drawings as
`draw:path`; recorded sound as the `draw:plugin` that media already
use; the gradients of several colours as the colour stops of
LibreOffice (`loext:gradient-stop`), with the two-colour gradient of
ODF as their fallback; the donut and radar charts as `chart:ring` and
`chart:radar` or `chart:filled-radar`, and the bubble chart as
`chart:bubble`. What ODF has no place for is an extension, defined in
[odf-extensions.md](../odf-extensions.md) in the pull request that adds
it, with its fallback: the image gallery (falling back to its first
image), 3D objects (to a picture of the model), the animation of a
drawing, the interactive chart (to its first data set), reference
lines, rounded corners, the bevel and the hole of a donut, and the shape
library saved by the person.
