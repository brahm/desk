## ODF

What ODF 1.3 names is kept in it: sections as `text:section` and master
pages; the fields of headers and footers as `text:page-number`,
`text:page-count`, `text:date`, `text:title` and the rest; the
background of a page as the `draw:fill` of its page layout or drawing
page; tables of contents as
`text:table-of-contents`, whose scope `text:index-scope` already names
the document or the chapter; bookmarks as `text:bookmark-start` and
`text:bookmark-end`; links of text as `text:a` and of objects as
`draw:a`; equations as MathML in an embedded object of
`application/vnd.oasis.opendocument.formula`, as LibreOffice Math
writes them; merge fields as `text:database-display`, with the source
in `text:database-name`. What ODF has no place for is an extension,
defined in [odf-extensions.md](../odf-extensions.md) in the pull request
that adds it, with its fallback: page templates and their tags, the
scope of a table of contents up to the next one, the LaTeX source of an
equation, the sender fields and the preview of a merge.
